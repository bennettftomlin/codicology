# Plan: what the five investigations say about the shape of the pipeline

12 November 2026. Written after five read-only investigations (tables, page furniture, unlinked
notes, equations, two-column adjudication) and a day of rules written the old way. Their reports and
data are in `/Users/Bennett1/claude_knowledge/rebuilds/2026-11-12-look/`. Nothing here is decided;
the order at the end is a proposal.

## The one thing they have in common

A build has two halves. The reading half works on typed blocks: each `PageItem` knows the layout's
name for it (Footnote, Table, PageHeader, Equation…), its box on the page, the recogniser's
confidence, and whether it is furniture. Then `_read_pages` flattens every page to one HTML string,
and the assembly half, about thirty passes in `build_epub` (867 lines), works on those strings with
patterns. None of them can see a label or a box.

Every finding of the last two days is something the assembly half had to re-derive, or could not:

| Finding | What it needed and did not have |
|---|---|
| Tables short of figures: 39 of 47 flagged are false | Where a table stands, in the PDF's own coordinates (the crop-box bug); which EPUB table is which cached block; that an index is not a table |
| Signature marks, page numbers run into the last line | Where on the page the block stands. Written today from text alone, and kept narrow because a loose rule is wrong nine times in ten |
| A running head under a designation | That the line above it is a PageHeader, and what kind |
| 4,525 unlinked note calls | Which blocks are footnotes (the label), which paragraphs are note entries, which raised figures are calls and which are exponents or table marks |
| Dispute pairs that are two different words | Which block each of our words came from, to hold against the witness's box |
| Words the reading passed over (11 November) | "The same block" had to be approximated by "the same text node", and the PDF asked again for positions |
| Binding (11 November) | Footnotes are guessed from a rule and a pattern, floats from tag names, captions from their first word |
| Display equations shown as raw TeX | No one place that decides how mathematics is written out |
| A link's address swallowing a paragraph | Model HTML parsed late, as a string, with nothing typed to refuse it |

`ReadPages` already carries sixteen fields, most of them side channels for what a string cannot
hold (`page_furniture`, `furniture_known`, `label_heads`, `toc_label_pages`, `foot_labelled`), and
each page drags four sidecar files (`.native`, `.layer.txt`, `.source.json`, `.key`). The model is
typed already; it is thrown away half way through.

## What the investigations found (short form)

- **Tables.** 896 typeset tables examined. Real or likely losses: 8 tables on 8 pages (2061 p69,
  935 p59, 1110 p262, 1123 p103, and four indexes set as tables). A bug: `_typeset_text_in` ignores
  the PDF's crop-box origin, so 121 tables in 30 books are measured in the wrong place; it also
  stops `mend_tables` taking 2061 p69, where a second reading with 132 of 132 figures already
  exists. The same arithmetic is in `_typeset_boxes_already_read`.
- **Furniture.** About 200 running heads, 198 of them in one book; 12 signature marks in two; 6
  fused page numbers in two. Rules written and measured today.
- **Notes.** 30,343 linked calls; 4,525 unlinked calls in 67 books, and 387 "unlinked" that are
  note-entry labels, not calls. Seven causes, each traced to a condition in the code. Three of them
  are vocabulary (a notes head the patterns do not know, a stray "1." entry, a front-matter
  "NOTE" counted as a notes head) and account for about 2,650 calls in five books. One book (4242)
  needs only a rebuild: 107. The rest are structural: one missing entry stops a whole group, feet
  without a rule under a table, two rules on a page.
- **Equations.** 429 display equations in 14 books, shown as raw TeX; 787 inline subscripts and
  120 powers left as `P_1`. MathML is core EPUB 3 (reading systems with a viewport MUST render
  Presentation MathML); `pandoc` converts 407 of the 429.
- **Two-column pages.** Not the cause. 45 of 45 disputes looked at are real. The artefacts are
  pairs of unlike words, about 1.6% of disputes, on prose as often as on columns. Six applied
  corrections were wrong and are retired; the long-s rule is written.

## How they interleave

Four things recur, and each is needed by more than one finding:

1. **One coordinate system.** Page fractions, PDF user space with its crop box, and pixels are
   converted by hand in at least four places. Needed by: tables check and mend, doubled boxes,
   put-back, any geometric furniture rule, the block test in adjudication.
2. **Block identity carried to the end.** Needed by: table matching, adjudication within a block,
   put-back ("same block"), binding (feet, floats), notes (footnote blocks), furniture rules.
3. **Notes as structure, not as passes over strings.** Calls per chapter and entries per group are
   two sequences to be aligned; today they are walked with `if n == expect`, so one missing entry
   or one stray one costs a chapter or a book.
4. **One writer.** How a block becomes XHTML (an equation as MathML or as text, a footnote, a page
   mark) decided in one place, with the manifest properties that go with it.

And one thing they all depend on for safety: **a shelf harness**. With `--cache-only` and the build
record (both written today) a rebuild is the cache and the code and nothing else, so "all 152 books
come out identical" can be a routine check. That is what makes moving code safe.

## The typed model, kept small

```
Book   pages
Page   index (PDF page), source (pdf, page, crop box, scan or typeset), folio, blocks, furniture
Block  kind (Paragraph, Heading, ListItem, Table, Figure, Caption, Footnote, NoteEntry,
             Equation, IndexEntry, TocEntry, Form…)
       label (the layout's own word, verbatim), box, conf, provenance, content
```

Two choices keep it tractable:

- **Blocks first, inline later.** A block's content stays an HTML fragment at first. That alone
  ends most of the pattern-matching over whole pages: "the page's footnotes" is the blocks whose
  kind is Footnote. Inline types (NoteCall, Math, Link) come only where they pay.
- **The cache does not change.** It already stores items with label, box and confidence. The model
  is what the cache holds, kept in memory to the end.

## Proposed order

**0. Now, the old way, because the yield is large and the code carries over.**
- Commit today's rules; install the six rebuilds when Calibre is closed.
- Crop-box fix with one conversion function; re-measure tables; rebuild the four books with a real
  table loss with the second reader (four pages).
- Notes: the three vocabulary rules (about 2,650 calls in 9482, 2102, 4116, 9559, 2085) and the
  rebuild of 4242. The predicates written for them are the ones a typed notes parser will use.
- Equations: inline subscripts and powers in `normalize_math` (no dependency). Display equations
  wait for the decision on MathML.

**1. The harness.** Move the staging and comparison tools from `rebuilds/` into the repo as one
command: build N books from cache with `--cache-only`, four at a time now that no server starts,
compare with a baseline, report. Target: the whole shelf in about half an hour.

**2. Split `pipeline.py` along its own banners, behaviour unchanged, checked by shelf identity.**
Capture and enhancement (about 1,900 lines, used only for video and photographs), the searchable
PDF, typography, citations, index first: nothing patches them. Then the witness functions into a
module of their own, which is 47 of the 72 places tests reach into the file. Then the second
reader, notes, contents and folios. `build_epub` last. `pipeline.py` stays as a facade so imports
keep working.

**3. Typed pages through assembly.** `_read_pages` returns pages of blocks and, for now, the same
strings rendered from them. Passes move over one at a time, output identical each time. Only then
do the rules that wanted geometry and labels get their better form: footnotes by label in the
binding, tables matched by identity, adjudication aligned within blocks, notes linked by aligning
two typed sequences (the structural causes), equations written by the writer.

## Open questions

- Display equations: MathML through pandoc (an optional dependency; rendering in Calibre's viewer
  and on Kindle not yet looked at), or plain text for the 372 simple ones, or both.
- Whether the notes rules of step 0 should go in before the harness, or after it as its first use.
- Decisions files address a word by its ordinal on the page. Any change that alters a page's token
  stream stales them; the harness has to check page text as well as files.
